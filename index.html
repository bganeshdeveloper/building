A FEW THINGS YOU MAY WONDER

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Where is the project located?

The reference project is located in the Panathur–Balagere neighbourhood of East Bengaluru. Enquire for the exact site entrance and directions.

Which home configurations can I explore?

The reference offers 2, 3, 3.5 and 4 BHK configurations. Contact the team to confirm current inventory and final areas.

How do I get the latest price sheet?

Use the enquiry form and select pricing as your interest. The team can share current prices, charges and availability.

Can I arrange a site visit?

Yes. Submit your contact details with a preferred visit date. A representative will need to confirm your appointment.

Where can I verify approvals and possession?

Request the official RERA registration, approvals and possession schedule from the developer before making a booking. These are not independently verified on this demonstration website.
